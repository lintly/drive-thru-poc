# Drive-Thru Vision AI — Competitor Feature Analysis

## 1. Fingermark (`Eyecue` Drive-Thru Performance)
- **Source:** https://fingermark.ai/drive-thru-performance
- **Core Positioning:** 98%-accurate end-to-end Vision AI that connects exterior drive-thru queues with interior back-of-house kitchen workstations.
- **Key Features:**
  - **5-Stage Ring Gauges:** Pre-Order, Order (COD 1 / COD 2), Payment, Deliver/Present, and Wait Bay.
  - **Parked / Wait Bay Accountability:** Per-bay top-down vehicle timers (Bays 1–3), Parked Car Avg (s), and % of cars parked vs. `<7%` target.
  - **OEPE% Tracking:** Last Hour % & Last Day % gauges, 5-hour trailing 15-min bar chart, Pull-Outs vs. daily limit, and Current 15-Min car target thermometer.
  - **FOH ↔ BOH Correlation & Trigger Alerts:** Monitors indoor stations (fry bay hopper %, beverage queue) and triggers real-time crew instructions (*"Pre-order queue is 5+ cars — Put more fries down"*, *"Wait bay: waiting 12 mins"*).
  - **Context Capture & Heatmaps:** Trajectory heatmaps plus tagged *Before / During / After* FOH+BOH clips for coaching.
  - **Daily Pulse & Network Leaderboards:** Mobile daily briefing with audio playback, weekly focus campaign, and multi-site OEPE leaderboards.

## 2. Berry AI (`AI Drive-Thru Timer` & `Berry Board`)
- **Source:** https://www.berry-ai.com/solutions/ai-drive-thru-timer/
- **Core Positioning:** Total exterior drive-thru visibility that eliminates loop blind spots, prevents "loop cheating" pull-forwards, and serves role-specific insights from frontline crew to brand executives.
- **Key Features:**
  - **Full-Property Stage Coverage:** Pre-Menu Queue, Dual Menu Boards (1 & 2), Production, Window, Pull-Ahead, Curbside pickup, Drive-Offs, and exterior handheld **Line-Busting** speed.
  - **Frontline Dark-Mode In-Store Monitor:** 6 high-contrast digital KPI tiles (*Queue Time*, *Cars in Queue* red alert at `6+`, *Menu Board 1 & 2*, *Car Count Difference vs. 2-wk avg*, *Today's Drive-Offs*), U-shaped dual-lane car tracker, and split-screen live AI camera feeds with `#0628xx` vehicle IDs.
  - **Store Manager (`Berry Board`):** Hourly Speed-of-Service vs. Drive-Off trends and **POS-matched vehicle logs** linking camera Vehicle IDs to order items and check totals.
  - **Brand Leadership BI:** Multi-quarter Speed-of-Service & Drive-Off reduction benchmarks plus an interactive Faster-Lane Revenue Calculator.
